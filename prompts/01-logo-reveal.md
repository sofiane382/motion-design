# Logo reveal

## Particules qui s'assemblent
Thousands of glowing golden particles swirl in a dark void, then converge and snap together to form a minimalist geometric logo at the center, subtle light sweep across the logo, slow dolly in, cinematic, deep black background, 5 seconds, ease-in-out.

## Encre liquide
A drop of black ink falls into milky white liquid in slow motion, the ink blooms and swirls, then reshapes into a clean sharp logo silhouette, top-down camera, high contrast, elegant, studio lighting.

## Extrusion chrome
A flat 2D logo outline draws itself with a neon line, then extrudes into a polished chrome 3D object that rotates 180 degrees and stops facing camera, reflective studio environment, soft blue rim light.
