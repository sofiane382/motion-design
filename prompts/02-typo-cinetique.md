# Typographie cinétique

## Mots qui rebondissent
Bold sans-serif white letters spelling "CRÉER" drop from above one by one onto a bright orange floor, each letter bounces with snappy overshoot and squash-and-stretch, flat 2D motion graphics style, front camera, clean background.

## Texte en tunnel
Endless tunnel made of repeating text rings reading "MOTION DESIGN", camera flies forward through the tunnel, black and electric lime colors, hypnotic, seamless loop.

## Lettres en papier
Paper-cut letters forming the word "HELLO" unfold and pop up like a pop-up book on a pastel desk, stop-motion feel, soft daylight, top-down shot.
