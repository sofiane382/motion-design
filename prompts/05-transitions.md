# Transitions

## Morph de formes
A red circle morphs into a blue square, then into a yellow triangle, then into a green star, smooth fluid shape morphing, flat 2D vector style, white background, snappy timing.

## Zoom infini
Camera zooms continuously into the pupil of an eye, which reveals a galaxy, which reveals a city at night, seamless infinite zoom transition, cinematic.

## Volet de couleur
Bold diagonal color bars (orange, purple, teal) sweep across the frame in quick succession, wiping to a clean black screen, flat motion graphics, energetic.
