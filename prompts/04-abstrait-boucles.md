# Abstrait / boucles

## Formes molles
Soft glossy 3D blobs in pastel colors squish, merge and separate on a clean background, satisfying motion, clay render style, seamless loop.

## Grille isométrique
Isometric grid of cubes rising and falling in a wave pattern, gradient from deep purple to cyan, minimal, smooth, seamless loop, top-down 45 degree camera.

## Fluide iridescent
Iridescent liquid metal flowing and folding in slow motion, holographic reflections, macro shot, dark background, elegant and hypnotic.
